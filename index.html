🍝 Alfredo Pasta Recipe

Ingredients

  • 250g Fettuccine Pasta
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 4 Garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 cup Fresh Cream
  • 1 cup Parmesan Cheese
  • Salt to taste
  • Black Pepper to taste
  • Parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente.
  2. Heat butter in a pan and sauté garlic for 1 minute.
  3. Add fresh cream and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  4. Mix in parmesan cheese until smooth.
  5. Add cooked pasta and toss well.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper.
  7. Garnish with parsley and serve hot.
